Texas TEFA questions

Is Literacy Arcade approved for Texas TEFA?

Yes. Literacy Arcade is an approved Texas TEFA provider, and Literacy Arcade Plus Annual and Plus Family Annual are both approved Odyssey offerings.

How do I purchase Literacy Arcade with TEFA funds?

Sign in through the official Texas TEFA portal, access Odyssey, search for Literacy Arcade, select the approved annual plan you want, and complete your purchase in Odyssey.

What happens after I purchase through Odyssey?

After Literacy Arcade receives your Odyssey order, watch for an email from hello@literacyarcade.com within 24 hours with directions for activating your subscription.

What does Plus Family include?

Plus Family includes the full Literacy Arcade Plus platform for children in one household, plus monthly literacy guidance and a live group Q&A with a reading specialist.

What is the difference between Plus and Plus Family?

Both include the same full Literacy Arcade Plus platform. Plus Family adds monthly literacy guidance and a live group Q&A for parents and guardians.

Can I purchase directly without using TEFA funds?

Yes. Families not using TEFA funds may purchase a direct Literacy Arcade Plus plan through the Plus pricing page.
