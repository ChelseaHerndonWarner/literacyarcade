How purchasing works with TheoPay

West Virginia Hope Scholarship families use TheoPay to submit their purchase. Here's how it works, in four steps.

Choose your product

Choose Plus Annual or The Letter Box from the West Virginia page.

Open the West Virginia checkout

Open the product's WV-specific checkout page in Google Chrome while signed in to TheoPay.

Submit with TheoPay

Use TheoPay to capture/scan the checkout information and submit the purchase request.

Hope Scholarship completes the order

The Hope Scholarship purchasing team reviews your request and completes the purchase with Literacy Arcade.

i

How payment works

For Plus Annual, Literacy Arcade will then set up your account for one full year of access. It does not automatically renew.

West Virginia Hope Scholarship questions

How do I purchase with Hope Scholarship funds?

Choose a product on this page, open its West Virginia checkout in Google Chrome while signed in to TheoPay, and use TheoPay to submit the purchase request.

Do I pay with my own credit card?

No. The West Virginia Hope Scholarship purchasing process completes the transaction with Literacy Arcade after you submit through TheoPay.

Which Literacy Arcade Plus plan is available?

Literacy Arcade Plus Annual, $79 for one full year of access.

Does the $79 plan renew automatically?

No. It's a one-time annual purchase. Funding may arrive quarterly, but Plus access is for one full year from activation, not quarterly.

What happens after the purchase is completed?

Literacy Arcade will set up your Plus access for one full year. Questions? Email hello@literacyarcade.com.

Can I purchase directly without using Hope Scholarship funds?

Yes. Families not using Hope Scholarship funds may purchase through the Plus pricing page.
