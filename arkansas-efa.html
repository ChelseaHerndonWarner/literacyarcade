Arkansas EFA questions

Can I use Arkansas EFA funds for Literacy Arcade?

Yes. Literacy Arcade is an approved Arkansas EFA vendor, and Arkansas EFA funds for the 2026–27 year are available now.

How do I pay Literacy Arcade through ClassWallet?

Generate your Literacy Arcade invoice, then upload it through the Arkansas EFA ClassWallet account for the student named on the purchase.

Has the Arkansas ClassWallet payment process changed?

ClassWallet now allows approved vendors to send payment requests to linked families as an alternative to an invoice. Literacy Arcade continues to use the invoice process: generate your Literacy Arcade invoice here, then upload it through the student's Arkansas EFA ClassWallet account.

How do I get an invoice?

Literacy Arcade generates it for you instantly. Request your invoice to get a PDF with the information needed for your ClassWallet upload.

Why does Plus Family still name one EFA student?

Arkansas EFA funds are tied to an individual student's EFA account. Plus Family is invoiced for the student named on that account, while the added Family benefits — monthly live group Q&A, help understanding assessment results, and guidance choosing tools and instructional next steps — support the parent or guardian.

What is the difference between Plus and Plus Family?

Both include the same full Literacy Arcade Plus platform. Plus Family adds monthly literacy guidance and a live group Q&A for the parent or guardian.

Can I purchase Literacy Arcade physical products with EFA funds?

Currently-available physical literacy products, like The Letter Box, can be added to an Arkansas EFA invoice for the named student. Eligible educational expenses remain subject to Arkansas EFA review.

Are Heart Word Flash Cards available to purchase?

Not right now — Heart Word Flash Cards are temporarily out of stock while more inventory arrives. You can view product details on the Shop page and join the waitlist to be notified when they're back.
