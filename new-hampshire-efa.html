New Hampshire EFA questions

Can I use New Hampshire EFA funds for Literacy Arcade?

Yes. Literacy Arcade is an approved Education Service Provider for the New Hampshire Education Freedom Account program.

How do I pay Literacy Arcade through ClassWallet?

Generate a Literacy Arcade invoice, then submit it through ClassWallet's Direct Pay process for the student named on your EFA account.

Do I need to pay out of pocket first?

No. ClassWallet Direct Pay lets you use EFA funds without paying Literacy Arcade out of pocket first.

Can I request reimbursement instead?

Yes. New Hampshire also allows reimbursement for eligible EFA expenses, though Direct Pay is the simpler path since it doesn't require paying out of pocket first.

How do I get an invoice?

Literacy Arcade provides it. Request your invoice and it will include the information needed for your ClassWallet submission.

Why does Plus Family still name one EFA student?

New Hampshire EFA funds belong to individual students. Plus Family is invoiced for the student named on the EFA account, while the added Family benefits provide guidance and support for the parent or guardian helping that student with reading.

What is the difference between Plus and Plus Family?

Both include the same full Literacy Arcade Plus platform. Plus Family adds monthly literacy guidance and a live group Q&A for the parent or guardian supporting the EFA student.

Can I purchase Literacy Arcade physical products with EFA funds?

Currently-available physical literacy products, like The Letter Box, can be added to a New Hampshire EFA invoice for the named student. Eligible educational expenses remain subject to New Hampshire EFA review.
