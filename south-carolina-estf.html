South Carolina ESTF questions

Can I use South Carolina ESTF funds for Literacy Arcade?

Yes. Literacy Arcade is an approved South Carolina ESTF provider, and eligible families can use ESTF funds for approved Literacy Arcade offerings.

How do I pay Literacy Arcade through ClassWallet?

Generate your South Carolina ESTF invoice on Literacy Arcade, then upload it through Pay Vendor in the student's ClassWallet account.

How do I get an invoice?

Literacy Arcade generates it for you instantly. Request your invoice to get a PDF with the information needed for your ClassWallet upload.

When will I receive access or my physical product?

Fulfillment occurs after Literacy Arcade receives approved payment — digital access is activated, or physical products are prepared for shipment, once payment is confirmed.

Can I purchase physical literacy products?

Currently-available approved physical products, like The Letter Box, can be included on a South Carolina ESTF invoice. Heart Word Flash Cards are temporarily out of stock and not currently available to invoice.

Why does Plus Family still name one ESTF student?

South Carolina ESTF funds are tied to an individual student's ESTF account. Plus Family is invoiced for the student named on that account, while the added Family benefits — monthly live group Q&A, help understanding assessment results, and guidance choosing tools and instructional next steps — support the parent or guardian.

What is the difference between Plus and Plus Family?

Both include the same full Literacy Arcade Plus platform. Plus Family adds monthly literacy guidance and a live group Q&A for the parent or guardian.

Are Heart Word Flash Cards available to purchase?

Not right now — Heart Word Flash Cards are temporarily out of stock while more inventory arrives. You can view product details on the Shop page and join the waitlist to be notified when they're back.
