/* Word Forge word bank — DRAFT FOR REVIEW (not used by the game yet).
   GENERATED by tools/word-forge/build-bank.js — edit the families there, not this file.

   A curated, offline list of words and their exact word-part (rune) sequences. When the game adopts
   it, forging will match the chain's part sequence against this bank — no dictionary lookups, no
   network — so only legitimate morphological constructions count and each round has a closed list
   of valid words (which lets the round end the moment the last one is found).

   Rules (enforced by tools/word-forge/validate-bank.js):
     • A word is exactly its parts joined in order, with NO spelling change (so no run+ing, write+ing,
       hope+ed). Words that need drop-e / doubling / y→i are simply not in the bank.
     • Parts follow the existing rune colors: zero or more prefixes, exactly one root, zero or more
       suffixes, in that order.
     • Accidental words (re + ed = "reed", un + less = "unless") are not in the bank, so they can't count.
     • Derivational suffixes come before inflectional ones (preschool+er+s, never s+er).
     • Words whose meaning is not transparent from their parts (discount, discover, recover, report, the
       transact family) are excluded. A dictionary hit alone is never enough: every family was chosen by hand.

   Shape
     parts   { id, text, type:'prefix'|'root'|'suffix', kind:'derivational'|'inflectional'|'base', meaning, level, origin, free }
       kind separates inflectional endings (-ed -ing -s -es) from derivational parts (prefixes, -er -ful -less
       -ness -ly -ment -ion -ation) so teacher customization can target them separately later.
     words   { w, parts:[partIds], level:1-4, review?, note? }
     recipes { id, vault:'builders'|'changers'|'chains'|'masters', tier:1-3, title, focus, parts:[partIds], minWords }
       A recipe is a round's rune set. Its valid words are COMPUTED: every bank word whose parts are all
       in the recipe. Nothing is hand-listed per round.

   Reserved for later (not implemented): Greek/Latin roots (origin:'latin'|'greek', variants such as
   scrib/script, combining vowels), a `spellingRule` on words that need a change, and teacher-chosen
   parts. `origin` and `free` (can the root stand alone as a word?) are recorded now so the data
   doesn't need reshaping. */
